# Gaz AI – Truth Detective
## User Guide

### 1. Getting started
Open the app and choose the section you want to use.

Gaz AI is designed to help you ask questions, investigate claims, collect evidence and explore connections between information.

### 2. Endless Investigation
Use the **Endless** section to explore a subject through a chain of questions.

1. Enter a question or subject.
2. Ask Gaz AI to investigate.
3. Read the answer.
4. Explore the suggested follow-up questions.
5. Choose another question to continue investigating.
6. Your investigation trail is saved locally so you can return to it.

**Tip:** Follow interesting leads, but don't assume every AI answer is proven fact.

### 3. Saving answers as evidence
When an AI answer contains useful information:

1. Open the answer.
2. Use the save-to-evidence option.
3. Check the saved record in the Evidence Ledger.
4. Keep the original question and answer together.

Duplicate protection helps prevent the same answer being saved repeatedly.

### 4. Evidence Ledger
The Evidence Ledger is where you keep information gathered during investigations.

Use it to:
- Review saved evidence.
- Keep track of useful information.
- Compare different pieces of evidence.
- Build a record of what you have discovered.

Remember to distinguish between confirmed facts, claims and theories.

### 5. Connections
Connections allow you to link two separate evidence records.

1. Open the Connections section.
2. Select two records.
3. Enter a reason explaining why they might be connected.
4. Save the connection.

A connection is a possible relationship to investigate, not automatic proof that two things are related.

### 6. Connection Map
Use the Connection Map to see how saved records are linked.

It can help you:
- Spot patterns.
- Follow possible relationships.
- See how different pieces of information fit together.
- Identify new questions to investigate.

### 7. Using AI responsibly
Gaz AI is an investigation assistant, not a fact-checking authority.

For reliable investigations:
- Check important claims against independent sources.
- Look for original documents and evidence.
- Consider alternative explanations.
- Keep opinions separate from verified facts.
- Be willing to change your mind when new evidence appears.

### 8. Saving your work
Your investigation trail and saved records are intended to help you continue your research.

Keep a backup of important information where possible, especially before reinstalling the app or changing devices.

### 9. Troubleshooting
If something doesn't work:

- **Text difficult to see:** Check that you are using the latest build.
- **Answer won't save:** Try again and check the Evidence Ledger.
- **Connection won't save:** Make sure you selected two different records and entered a reason.
- **App closes unexpectedly:** Note what you were doing and check for an updated build.

### The purpose of Gaz AI
Ask questions. Follow evidence. Challenge assumptions. Find connections. Keep investigating.
